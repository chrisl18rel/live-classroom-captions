AI Mode

Connect your OpenAI account

AI Mode is optional. It swaps the free built-in recognition for OpenAI's transcription, which handles technical terms and student accents more accurately. It runs on your own OpenAI account and bills by the minute, so you stay in control of the spend.

Browser mode

Free, built in

Uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Nothing to set up, no account, no cost. Great for everyday speech.

AI mode

More accurate, your key

Streams audio to OpenAI for higher accuracy with technical vocabulary and other languages. Billed to your own OpenAI key by the minute.

Connect your key

  1. Create an account at platform.openai.com (or sign in if you already have one).
  2. Add a payment method under Settings → Billing. AI Mode needs billing enabled; it isn't available on the free trial tier.
  3. Go to API keys, click Create new secret key, and copy it. OpenAI shows the key only once, so copy it before closing the dialog.
  4. In the extension, open Settings & styling → Speech Recognition, choose AI Mode, paste your key, and click Save settings.
  5. Start captions as usual. AI Mode now handles the transcription.
Cap your spend: in OpenAI's Billing → Limits, set a monthly usage limit (for example, $10). OpenAI stops once you hit it, so there are no surprises. Your key is stored only on your own computer and is sent only to OpenAI.

Privacy

In Browser mode, audio is processed on your own device and never leaves it. In AI Mode, audio is streamed to OpenAI under your own key to produce the transcript; the extension never records or stores it, and never sends it to the developer. See the privacy policy for details.
